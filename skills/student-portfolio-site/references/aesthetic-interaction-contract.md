# Aesthetic Interaction Contract

Read this before asking a student to make a visual choice. The objective is a low-friction decision with visible ownership, not a design lecture.

## Standard choice message

After the material and public boundary are known, present three concise, genuinely different directions. Give every direction the same fields:

| Field | What to state |
| --- | --- |
| Direction name | A memorable plain-language name. |
| First impression | The feeling a visitor should have in the first screen. |
| Reading rhythm | Sparse/editorial, compact/systematic, or another clear density choice. |
| Visual language | Type scale, color/material, image treatment, and component character. |
| Interaction | What motion or interaction supports the direction, plus what will be avoided. |
| Fit | Why it fits this student's target role and available evidence. |

Then always append this fourth option in Chinese conversations:

```text
④ 我的审美比你牛，我来上传
```

For other languages, use a playful and respectful equivalent. Do not frame this as a fallback or a failure to choose.

## When the student chooses the fourth option

Invite any combination of screenshots, links, a mood board, and a short description of what they like. If they cannot explain it, accept that. Do not make them learn visual terminology.

Analyse the submitted material before building. Give the student a compact mapping:

| Reference | Visible trait or stated preference | Principle to apply | Planned portfolio decision | Deliberately not copying |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Screenshot/link name | e.g. generous type and asymmetric reading flow | strong editorial hierarchy | home-page hero and case-study titles | brand marks, text, images, and full layout |

Distinguish what is visible in a screenshot from what can only be verified from an accessible link. Do not republish private screenshots, copy brand assets, or claim unobserved responsive or motion behavior.

## Fast path

If the student replies “你决定”“我不会选”“就按推荐做”, recommend one direction with a one-sentence reason tied to their target role and evidence. Ask for one lightweight confirmation, then build. Do not require them to inspect additional public references first; keep the option to upload their own reference open for later.

If the student already provides a detailed design brief or a decisive reference set, skip the three AI directions. Summarize the inferred brief, provide the reference map, ask for a lightweight confirmation, and proceed.

## Greenlight to build

Before global styles, visual components, imagery treatment, or motion are built, ensure the student has confirmed either:

- one of the three directions;
- the fast-path recommendation; or
- the reference-led brief.

The confirmation can be as short as “就按这个做”. Preserve it through later iterations unless the student asks to reconsider the visual direction.
